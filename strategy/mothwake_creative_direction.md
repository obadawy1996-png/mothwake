# MOTHWAKE creative direction (proposal, awaiting owner review)

**Status:** PLANNED. Based on `research/competitor_comparison.md` (2026-11-20) and the earlier short-form research (`reports/SHORTFORM_RECOMMENDATION.md`). Nothing here changes approved decisions until the owner approves it.

## The hypothesis tested: should MOTHWAKE blend in psychology, money and documentary?
- **Money and power:** **reject as a pillar.** It's off-brand for "dark animated horror" and would dilute the identity. The real-world fact risk is high (Money Decoded shows it).
- **Human psychology:** **adopt as a layer, not a topic switch.** Horror *is* psychology: sleep paralysis, the feeling of being watched, false memories, why the brain fills darkness with shapes. tavieenfilm proves "fiction story + real studies" earns trust and views.
- **Documentary framing:** **adopt the frame devices** (countdown clock, investigation, rooms) for long-form only.

## Recommended direction
**"Original horror stories built on real fear science."** Every story is fiction; where a real psychological effect is involved, the end card names it and cites a verified source. This becomes a recognisable, defensible MOTHWAKE signature that no template channel has.

| Item | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | English, 16–34, US/UK/CA/AU (the earlier research markets) | Matches the Shorts horror audience; the tavieenfilm audience overlaps (teen/young adult) |
| Format | Shorts 20–45 s (discovery) + long-form 7–11 min "Investigations" every 3–4 weeks (watch hours) | Same as the approved hybrid; tavieenfilm shows 7–11 min story-docs can break out |
| Pillars (5) | 1 **Micro horror** (sleep, home, everyday objects) · 2 **Rules** (original-world, family) · 3 **Choose-your-door** interactive (A/B, Part 2 by vote) · 4 **Fear Science**: a horror story + a real effect named at the end · 5 **Investigations** (long-form, countdown/suspects frame) | Pillars 1–3 are already evidence-backed; 4–5 are borrowed *mechanics* with an original subject |
| Visual identity | Keep the approved MOTHWAKE look (ash violet, old gold, one warm light). Add **giant brand-type number/time cards** (Cinzel) and **clock-time chapter cards** | Strong at phone size; copies no one's style |
| Narration | Second person present tense for Shorts ("You hear it again"), first person Ivy for recurring-world stories. Short declarative sentences, at most 12 words | Immersion plus caption readability |
| Titles | Question or reversal; a family or home word; no 3AM/"DON'T" (earlier data) | Research-backed outlier features |
| Thumbnails | One wrong detail + at most 4 words + brand moth mark | Existing THUMBNAIL_SYSTEM.md |
| Honesty line | Long-form end card: "The story is fiction. The [effect] is real." plus sources in the description | Trust; avoids misleading real-world claims |

## Sample original concept (illustrative only; not approved, not produced)
**"Why You Feel Watched Before You Open Your Eyes"** · Fear Science pillar · Short, about 40 s (long-form "Investigation" version possible later).
- **Hook (0–3 s):** black screen, a giant card **"2:14"**. VO: "You wake up. Eyes still shut. You already know someone's in the room."
- **Escalation:** clock cards 2:14 → 2:15 → 2:16. Each one cuts to the bedroom from a slightly closer angle. A floorboard. The duvet dips at the foot of the bed.
- **Rewind device:** "Go back to 2:14. Same room. This time, open your eyes." A split screen: left, eyes closed (the dip moves closer); right, eyes open (an empty room… a moth on the lamp).
- **Twist:** the right side's moth lands on the lamp, and the left side's lamp is *off*. "Only one of these rooms is yours."
- **End card:** "The story is fiction. The feeling of being watched in your sleep is real." Name the effect and cite a source, **only after the source is verified** (not done yet).
- **CTA:** "Eyes open or eyes shut — which room were you in?"
- **Production:** 5 Flow Lite clips (the existing Ivy bedroom refs), 1 split-screen composite in FFmpeg, type cards in brand fonts. Estimate: about 50 Flow credits + about 250 ElevenLabs credits.

## Update 2026-11-20 after adding Paper Billions
The recommendation is unchanged: **original horror + real fear science, human-directed.** Paper Billions' success comes from real-world finance and espionage narration built by a disclosed automated AI pipeline. Following it would mean real-people fact risk, off-brand topics, and the mass-produced-content policy risk the brief forbids. From it we take only mechanics: a dramatic cold open, physical metaphors, a recurring evidence-board visual (redrawn in MOTHWAKE style) and a recurring anchor character (Ivy).
