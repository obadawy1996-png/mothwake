## Proportions
- 6.5 heads tall (house adult standard). Lanky, slightly long arms and fingers (the "moth" stylisation, kept subtle).
- Shoulders a little rounded and forward, from years of leaning into a microphone.
- Neck slightly elongated. Head slightly oval, not round.
- Eyes large and simple (almond shape), brows thick and very mobile: they carry most of his acting.

## Silhouette notes (what makes him readable in solid black)
1. **Over-ear headphones always around his neck** — a ring shape at the collar with two cup bumps. This is his main silhouette marker.
2. **Cowlick** at the crown: one tuft of short hair sticks up and back.
3. **Field jacket with a bunched hood** behind the collar, making a soft hump at the back of the neck.
4. A small rectangular **handheld recorder** in his right hand in most scenes.
5. Forward lean when he listens (head ahead of shoulders).

## Expressions (brows and mouth do the work)
| Expression | Notes |
|---|---|
| Neutral | Easy half-smile, brows level. His default in Act I. |
| Curious | One brow up, head tilted forward, mouth slightly open as if about to ask. |
| Uneasy | Brows pinched together, smile still there but stiff. |
| Afraid | Eyes wide, brows up and together, mouth pressed shut (he is trying not to speak). |
| Terror | Eyes very wide, hand over mouth. Never a scream (silence is the rule). |
| Relief | Shoulders drop, eyes close, slow exhale. |
| Blank | Flat face, eyes unfocused. Used in Act III after no sleep. |

## Signature poses
- Holding the recorder out toward someone, arm extended, body leaning back slightly (polite but pushy).
- Headphones on, hunched over the laptop, one hand pressed to an ear cup.
- Sitting on the motel bed surrounded by paper notes.
- Driving, lit from below by the dashboard.

## Wardrobe
Same outfit all three days (he only packed for one night): olive-grey canvas field jacket, charcoal hoodie, dark jeans, worn brown boots, teal headphones around the neck. Night three: jacket off, hoodie only, hair flatter, darker eye shadows.

## Consistency risks
- Generators like to put headphones *on* the head. Always prompt "headphones resting around his neck" unless the shot says otherwise.
- Teal can drift toward bright cyan. Reject any take where the headphones look saturated.
- Cal appears in none of the 12 hero Flow clips; his shots are planned as layered stills. This lowers the risk of drift.
