## Proportions
- 6.5 heads tall, compact and solid, very upright posture. Moves little; when she moves, it is deliberate.
- Long, elegant fingers (the moth stylisation shows most in her hands, because her hands do her talking).
- Face: square jaw, heavy-lidded steady eyes, strong straight brows.

## Silhouette notes
1. **Low bun with a pencil pushed through it** — a cross shape at the back of the head. Main marker.
2. **Notepad on a string around her neck** — a small rectangle hanging at the sternum.
3. Knee-length apron: a straight vertical edge from waist to knee.
4. Very straight, still stance; arms often folded or one hand flat on the counter.

## Expressions (she never opens her mouth)
| Expression | Notes |
|---|---|
| Neutral | Mouth closed, level gaze straight at the viewer. Unsettlingly calm. |
| Curious | Very slight brow lift. Nothing else moves. |
| Uneasy | Lips pressed, eyes slide toward the window. |
| Afraid | Small fast head shake, eyes wide, hand to the notepad. |
| Terror | Eyes closed tight, hand over her own mouth. |
| Relief | Long slow blink, shoulders drop a little. |
| Blank | Unfocused eyes, face slack, staring at the radio. Used in the ending. |

**Rule for generation:** her lips stay closed in every shot. Reject any take where her mouth moves as if speaking.

## Signature poses
- Writing on the notepad and turning it toward the viewer (page blank in the image).
- Tearing a page off, folding it in half, pocketing it.
- Pointing: at Cal, at the door, at the road (three-beat gesture).
- Sitting in the dark beside the radio, hand stopped above the dial.

## Wardrobe
Work: plum-charcoal cardigan, bone collared shirt, cranberry apron, notepad on a cranberry string, pencil in the bun.
Ending (her flat): same cardigan, no apron. **Hair stays in the bun with the pencil** and the notepad stays on its string, so the silhouette is identical to her diner look and the same reference `REF-CH-INES-TURN` works for the ending clip.

## Consistency risks
- Generators often add a name badge or text on the apron. Prompt "plain apron, no badge, no text".
- The notepad must stay blank. Any letters on it in a take mean reject or repaint.
- In the ending (dark flat, radio light from below) the cranberry accent reads almost black. Recognition there relies on the bun-and-pencil and the notepad silhouette.
