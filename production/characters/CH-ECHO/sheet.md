## Allowed appearances (EP001)
1. **Breath fog on glass, from the outside.** A soft oval bloom of condensation at head height, as if someone is standing very close to the window. It grows over about 3 s and fades over about 4 s. Nothing is visible through it except darkness.
2. **A silhouette behind frosted or dark glass**, edges soft, never sharp, never lit from the front.
3. **An implied presence at the edge of light** (a doorway, a hallway) — a darker patch in the dark, and nothing more.
4. Sound from the motel wall, the phone, the radio and the hallway — no picture needed.

## Wrong proportions (when a shape is shown)
- About **9 heads tall** (people are 6.5): it is too tall for the window frame, so the top of it is often cropped.
- **Head too small** for the body and tilted to one side, as if listening.
- **Shoulders too narrow, arms too long**: the hands would hang past the knees.
- Neck slightly too long.
- It does not move while watched. Between cuts it is closer.

## Animation notes
- Fog bloom: ease-in 3 s, hold 1 s, fade 4 s. Very slight irregular "breathing" pulse during the hold.
- Silhouette: no motion inside a shot except a very slow head tilt (under 5°).
- Best built in compositing (fog as an FX layer on a still window), which is also the fallback for the Flow clip.

## Consistency risks (highest of any EP001 element)
- Generators want to show a monster. Reject any take with a face, teeth, mouth, eyes, claws, a clear figure, or gore.
- Fog may render as text-like smears or a handprint. A handprint is acceptable only if owner approves; text is never acceptable.
- If a take shows too much, cut it or cover it with the compositing fog layer. Less is always safer.
