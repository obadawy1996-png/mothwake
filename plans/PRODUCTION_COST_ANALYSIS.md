# Production Cost Analysis — 30-Day Pilot

Prices are USD list prices from `research/TOOL_STACK_VERIFICATION.md`, checked 2026-11-19 via official-domain search extracts. **Re-check each pricing page before buying.** Usage figures are estimates and are labelled as such.

## Usage estimate for the pilot (4 long-form + 14 Shorts)

| Item | Assumption (estimate) | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| Narration characters | Long-form 12 min × 145 wpm ≈ 1,740 words ≈ 10k characters; Shorts ≈ 750 characters each | 40k + 10.5k = **≈ 50k characters** |
| Retakes / alternative reads | ×2 | **≈ 100k ElevenLabs credits** (about 1 credit per character, per pricing page) |
| Animated "hero" clips (Flow/Veo) | Long-form 12 each; Shorts 3 each. Most shots are layered parallax stills from our own pipeline. | 90 clips × 2 attempts = **≈ 180 generations** |
| Flow credits | Veo 3.1 Lite 10 credits / Fast 20 credits per generation | Lite ≈ 1,800, Fast ≈ 3,600 |
| Clipping | Shorts cut from long-form | 4 long-form ≈ 50 min processed |

## Monthly cost scenarios

| Scenario | Stack | USD / month |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Lean (recommended start)** | ElevenLabs **Creator** USD 22 (121k credits, commercial) + Google AI **Pro** USD 19.99 (1,000 Flow credits ≈ 100 Lite gens) + Resolve Free + FFmpeg pipeline + Metricool Free | **≈ 42** |
| A + clipping | + OpusClip Starter USD 15 (commercial use; whether it removes the watermark is unverified) | ≈ 57 |
| B — Heavier animation | ElevenLabs Creator USD 22 + Google AI **Ultra** USD 99.99 (10,000 credits) + OpusClip Starter USD 15 | ≈ 137 |
| One-off option | DaVinci Resolve Studio USD 295 (enables scripting); not needed for the pilot | one-time |

**Constraint in scenario A:** 1,000 Flow credits cover about 100 Lite generations, which is about half the estimated 180. Either animate fewer hero shots (lean harder on the parallax pipeline, which costs nothing per shot) or buy top-up credits. Decide after week 1 using the real attempts-per-usable-clip rate.

**Free tiers don't work for publishing:** ElevenLabs Free is non-commercial with attribution, OpusClip and Magnific free plans are non-commercial, and Flow free-tier commercial terms are unverified.

## Cost per published minute (scenario A, estimate)

About 4 × 12 + 14 × 0.85 ≈ 60 published minutes, so **≈ USD 0.70 per finished minute** in tool costs. That excludes your time and your review.
