# THE LAST SAVE (MOTHWAKE long-form, YouTube 16:9, target ≤ 10 min)

Original fiction. The game SUNSHORE is invented; no real game, studio, character, map, logo or music is used or named. Narrator: Ivy (Ana-Rita2, eleven_v4). Jun (15) is always alone in frame; Ivy appears only in her own shots.
Structure: cold open → title → NIGHT ONE … NIGHT FIVE → DAY SIX (Comfort Turn: the warm thing is the threat). End: an open choice for the audience.

## P0 · Cold open
This is Jun's save file. [pause] Day six. [long pause] The game is called Sunshore. Sunshine, palm trees, a city by the sea. [pause] You can drive anywhere. You can go anywhere. [long pause] But the last place Jun saved... is here. [long pause] Our building. Third floor. [long pause] [whispers] My door. [long pause] I'm Ivy. I live next to Jun. And I want to tell you how his game found its way home... before I decide whether to press continue.

## P1 · NIGHT ONE
Night one. [pause] Jun is fifteen. His mum works nights at the hospital, so most nights it's just him, the sofa, and the TV. [pause] I hear it through the wall. Usually that's fine. [long pause] He queued till midnight for the new game. I heard him come up the stairs two at a time. [long pause] The first screen is a postcard of the city. Sunshine. Palm trees. The sea. [long pause] And in the corner, where nobody would look... a grey building. [long pause] Our building. [pause] Third floor. His window. The light on. [long pause] He laughed about it the next morning. 'They must have used a photo,' he said. 'Some designer lives round here.' [long pause] I asked him who was standing in the window. [long pause] [whispers] He hadn't noticed anyone.

## P2 · NIGHT TWO
Night two. [pause] Everyone online is hunting the ghost car. A white car, no driver, that only shows up at night. People post blurry clips and argue about where it appears. [long pause] Jun found it on day one. [long pause] Not in the game. [long pause] At two in the morning he knocked on my wall. Three knocks. Our signal for: come and look. [long pause] From my window I could see it too. In our car park. Under the dead streetlamp. A white car, engine running, headlights on. [long pause] Nobody inside. [long pause] And from somewhere in the car, very quietly... the music from his save screen. [long pause] By morning the bay was empty. No tyre marks. Just a dry rectangle on the wet tarmac, the exact shape of a car.

## P3 · NIGHT THREE
Night three. [pause] Jun started writing rules. That's what you do when a game has rules nobody tells you. [long pause] One: never let your character sleep. [pause] Two: if the radio plays a lullaby, save and quit. [pause] Three: never pause on a dark street. [long pause] Four... [long pause] [whispers] if your character lies down, close your own eyes too. [long pause] I asked him why. He didn't look up. [long pause] [whispers] So it doesn't close them for you. [long pause] And here's the part I keep coming back to. In Sunshore, the people in the streets had started keeping our rules. The ones from this building. They pressed the light switch once. They walked, never ran. When the lights went out, they stood still. [long pause] Nobody taught them that. [pause] Somebody must have been watching us.

## P4 · NIGHT FOUR
Night four. [pause] The game gave Jun a choice. A figure at the door of a little pastel house. Two options. Let him in. Or keep playing. [long pause] Then a knock. [long pause] Not in the game. [long pause] On his front door. Same rhythm. Three slow knocks. A pause. Three more. [long pause] I was on the landing. I could see his door. [long pause] There was nobody on the other side. Just the light under it... and in the light, the shadow of two feet. [long pause] Jun didn't choose. He stood between the TV and the door with his thumb over the button until the sun came up. [long pause] That morning I noticed something about the game. Every night, the sun in Sunshore set a little earlier. [pause] The palm trees were turning into streetlamps.

## P5 · NIGHT FIVE
Night five. [pause] Jun found the secret ending. [long pause] Past the beach. Past the pier. Past the edge of the map, where the painted world just... stops. [long pause] A grey building. Ours. [long pause] Third floor. A moth on the window. Old gold wings. [long pause] He pressed save. [long pause] And the real moth landed on his screen. [long pause] He called me in. He was smiling, the way you smile when you're too tired to be scared. [long pause] It's not an ending, he said. [long pause] [whispers] It's a save point. For the building.

## P6 · DAY SIX
Day six. [pause] His mum came home and couldn't wake him. Not ill. Not hurt. Just asleep, deeply asleep, with the controller in his hands. The doctors say he's fine. That he's dreaming. [long pause] The console was still on. One save file. Jun. Day six. [long pause] I pressed continue. [long pause] The game opened on our hallway. Our light. My door. [long pause] And in front of my door, a small figure in a dark hoodie with lime-green cords... waiting. [long pause] Then, here, in the real world... a knock. On my door. [pause] Three slow knocks. A pause. Three more. [long pause] [whispers] The game is asking me to choose. [long pause] Let him in. Or keep playing. [long pause] I haven't decided. [long pause] What would you do? [long pause] [whispers] Some things should stay asleep.
