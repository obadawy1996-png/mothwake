# EP001 — Hero clip prompts (Google Flow, Ingredients to Video)

**Status:** draft for owner, 2026-11-19. Nothing generated. The owner generates manually in Flow.
**Start only after** all ingredients a clip needs are approved (`REFERENCE_IMAGES.md`).

## Fixed settings for every clip
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Mode | Ingredients to Video |
| Aspect | 16:9 |
| Length | 8 s |
| Outputs per prompt | 1 |
| Style | `STYLE_PROMPT.md` v1 (already assembled into every prompt below) |
| Audio | Generated audio is muted/replaced in post. The Echo's voice is never generated in Flow. |
| Credit cost | Owner brief: **Lite 10, Fast 20 credits per generation** (owner-supplied figures, not checked by Claude). Confirm the number Flow shows before clicking Generate. |

**Text rule:** every sign, pad page, screen, clock display and tape label is generated blank. The words come in post (Inter / Cinzel overlays) in the Video Assembly step.

**Model choice rule:** Lite for slow, mostly static shots (lights, fog, camera drift). Fast for shots with hands, liquid, a flying moth or a character acting, where quality matters more.

## Fallback: the "parallax still" plan (applies to every clip)
If a clip fails twice (style drift, text appears, wrong character, gore, Echo shown too much), **stop spending credits on it** and build it as a parallax still instead:
1. Take the best approved still for that shot (the listed background ingredient, or the best single frame of a failed take if it is on-model).
2. Split it into 4–6 layers (sky/far, mid, near, foreground silhouette, light FX) per the Environment Bible; store in `production/backgrounds/<id>/` with a layer JSON.
3. Animate in the assembly step with the approved motion recipes (parallax drift up to 4 % scale over 6–10 s, slow push-in, flicker, dust specks, moth flutter loop).
The per-clip notes below say which layers and recipes to use.

### C01 — Coffee overflows as the diner freezes (Cold open)
**Model:** Veo 3.1 Fast · **Credits:** 20 · **Aspect:** 16:9 · **Ingredients:** REF-BG-DINER-INT, REF-PROP-COFFEE-CU, REF-STYLE-01
```
MOTHWAKE style. Dark 2D hand-drawn animation, flat cel shading with only two tones per surface (base and shadow) and one soft warm rim light from the single light source in the scene. Strictly limited palette: near-black obsidian, charcoal, muted ash violet, dull old gold, bone white, and one warm lamp-yellow light. Cool ash-violet darkness surrounds one warm pool of light. Painted flat backgrounds with no outlines and soft simplified edges away from the focal point; characters drawn with a thin dark charcoal line. Small warm lamp-coloured highlights in every eye. Quiet, still, atmospheric night. Restrained motion, slow camera. 16:9 cinematic frame. Close shot at a diner counter at night. A hand holds a glass coffee pot and pours steadily into a plain white cup on a saucer. Behind, soft out-of-focus shapes of two men in a booth, mid-laugh, and the round wall clock with no numbers, its second hand reaching one o'clock. At the moment the second hand lands, everything stops: the people behind are frozen still like a held drawing, steam hangs in the air, but the coffee keeps pouring. The cup fills, overflows over the rim and spreads slowly across the counter in a dark shining pool. The hand never moves. Camera locked off, very slow push-in toward the cup. Warm lamp-yellow pendant light from above, ash-violet shadows. Audio: quiet room tone only, no speech, no voices, no music. Avoid: photorealism, 3D render, CGI, glossy plastic look, anime, chibi, bright or saturated colours, neon, multiple coloured light sources, heavy lens flare, blood, gore, wounds, corpses, monsters with faces, teeth or claws, text, letters, numbers, words, captions, subtitles, signage lettering, logos, brand names, watermarks, readable screens, extra fingers, distorted hands, people speaking or moving their lips, camera shake, fast cuts.
```
**Fallback (parallax still):** layers = background booth silhouettes (frozen), clock, counter, cup+pot, coffee spill FX. Animate a spill mask growing over 6 s in compositing; slow push-in. Steam as a still overlay.

## Review checklist for each take (before it is kept)
- [ ] Matches `REF-STYLE-01` (flat cel, two tones, one rim light, limited palette). No style flicker between frames.
- [ ] No text or pseudo-text anywhere; no logos; phone screen blank.
- [ ] Characters on-model (accent colour, silhouette markers, Lamp eye highlights); lips do not move.
- [ ] Echo: no face, eyes or clear body.
- [ ] No gore.
- [ ] Logged in the generation log (model, credits, date, account, take, kept / rejected and why).
