# EP001 — Step 1: reference stills (the Ingredients)

**Status:** draft for owner, 2026-11-19. Nothing here has been generated. Claude prepared the prompts only; the owner generates in Flow in their own browser.

## Why stills come first
Flow's Ingredients mode takes **up to 3 reference images per clip** (Veo 3.1 Lite or Fast only). Clips stay consistent only if every clip is fed the same small set of **approved** stills. So we make, review and approve these stills before any clip is generated.

## Order of work
1. **Make `REF-STYLE-01` first.** Generate it, pick the best take, and get owner approval. This frame defines the MOTHWAKE look for the whole episode. If it is wrong, everything after it will be wrong.
2. If Flow's image tool accepts a reference image (**unknown**, check in the UI), attach the approved `REF-STYLE-01` to every other still below.
3. Make the **Step 1a** stills (required for the 12 hero clips). Approve each against its character sheet or the Environment Bible.
4. Make **Step 1b** stills (optional; used for layered stills, not hero clips) only if credits allow.
5. Save each approved still as `episodes/EP001/art/flow/refs/<REF-ID>_v<n>.png`, and copy character refs to `production/characters/<CH-ID>/refs/`. Record each one in the generation log (see `EXPORT_CHECKLIST.md`).

## Credit cost of still images
**Unknown.** The owner brief gives Veo clip costs only. Before the first still, check the cost shown in Flow's image tool. If stills cost credits, update `CREDIT_BUDGET.md` before going on. Alternative at zero Flow cost: the owner paints or edits the stills by hand and uploads them as ingredients.

## Settings for every still
- Aspect 16:9, one output per prompt.
- Full prompt = style prefix + shot text + avoid line, from `STYLE_PROMPT.md` (v1). The full prompts below are already assembled. Copy the whole code block.
- Everything written on signs, pads, screens, clocks and labels is left **blank** and added in post.

```
MOTHWAKE style. Dark 2D hand-drawn animation, flat cel shading with only two tones per surface (base and shadow) and one soft warm rim light from the single light source in the scene. Strictly limited palette: near-black obsidian, charcoal, muted ash violet, dull old gold, bone white, and one warm lamp-yellow light. Cool ash-violet darkness surrounds one warm pool of light. Painted flat backgrounds with no outlines and soft simplified edges away from the focal point; characters drawn with a thin dark charcoal line. Small warm lamp-coloured highlights in every eye. Quiet, still, atmospheric night. Restrained motion, slow camera. 16:9 cinematic frame. Wide establishing shot at night: a small-town diner alone on an empty main street, every other building dark. The diner's big front windows glow with warm lamp-yellow light that spills onto the wet pavement. Inside, soft shapes of booths and a counter. The street and the sky are deep ash violet fading to near-black. Far behind the rooftops, on a low hill, a thin radio tower with one tiny red light at the top. Plain blank sign board above the diner door. Calm, lonely, beautiful and slightly wrong. Avoid: photorealism, 3D render, CGI, glossy plastic look, anime, chibi, bright or saturated colours, neon, multiple coloured light sources, heavy lens flare, blood, gore, wounds, corpses, monsters with faces, teeth or claws, text, letters, numbers, words, captions, subtitles, signage lettering, logos, brand names, watermarks, readable screens, extra fingers, distorted hands, people speaking or moving their lips, camera shake, fast cuts.
```
