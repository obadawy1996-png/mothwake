**TITLE: FIVE MOTHS**
**I · ONE LAST JOB.** They called themselves Rae and Dez. One last job, the way it always is. [pause] The beach casino keeps its night safe in an office with a window onto the sea. [long pause] They were back on the coast road in four minutes. Clean. [long pause] Dez laughed the whole way. Rae didn't. [long pause] Because sitting on the outside of the windscreen, right in front of her eyes, was a moth. [pause] Old gold wings. Perfectly still at sixty miles an hour.
**II · TWO.** They ditched the car behind a motel and took another one. A white estate with the keys left in. [long pause] Windows up. Doors locked. [long pause] Two miles later, there was a moth on the inside of the windscreen. [pause] And the first one was back on the outside. [long pause] Dez turned on the radio to drown out the quiet. **III · THE RULES OF THE COAST ROAD.** Everybody who grows up in Palmer Key knows the rules. [pause] Don't drive after the streetlamps go out. [pause] Don't take the bridge at three. [pause] And if a moth lands on you... pay for something. Anything. Honesty puts it back to sleep. [long pause] Rae and Dez didn't grow up here. [long pause] They took the bridge at three. [long pause] Behind them, one by one, the streetlamps went out. [long pause] Three moths.
**IV · THE TOWN WAKES UP.** At half past three, Palmer Key started turning its lights on. [pause] Not the streetlamps. The houses. [long pause] Every window they passed lit up, one after another, like a wave following the car. And in every window, someone in a dressing gown. [long pause] Not angry. Not shouting. Just standing. [long pause] For a second she reached for her purse. [long pause] Then Dez leaned on the horn. [long pause] She took a chocolate bar from the rack, put it in her pocket, and walked out without paying. [long pause] [whispers] Five moths.
**VI · THE LAST PULL-IN.** I didn't hear the car leave. [long pause] When I looked up, it was still at pump two. Engine running. Radio playing the lullaby. Doors closed. [long pause] Nobody inside. [long pause] On each front seat, a moth. Old gold wings. Perfectly still. [long pause] I went to turn the engine off. And in the rear-view mirror I saw the back seat. [long pause] Full.
