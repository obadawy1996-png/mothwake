# SH019 Theo's Nightlight Rules — clip prompts v2 (child and adult in separate shots)

**Status:** 2026-11-20, owner approved the rewrite. **This replaces the SH019 section of CLIP_PROMPTS.md (v1 is kept unchanged for the record).**

**Why:** on Chrono Tales, Flow refused frames and prompts showing a minor and an adult interacting, and child image uploads (see FLOW_SAFETY_NOTES.md). In v2, **Theo and Ivy never share a frame and never touch**. Their relationship comes from eyelines and the cut (the Kuleshov approach). No prompt is reworded to get past a refusal.

**REF-THEO\*:** make REF-THEO **inside Flow** with Nano Banana (0 credits) and try it as an ingredient on C2a. If Flow refuses it, drop it and run C2a with the text description only. Never upload an outside child image.

**Cost (estimate):** 10 clips, 9 Lite + 1 Fast = **110 credits first pass, 220 max** (v1 was 80/160). The whole pilot becomes **220 first pass / 440 max** (44% of 1,000 a month).
