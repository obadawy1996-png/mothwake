### 1. REF-STYLE-01V — Style frame (make first)
```
MOTHWAKE style. Dark 2D hand-drawn animation, flat cel shading with only two tones per surface (base and shadow) and one soft warm rim light from the single light source in the scene. Strictly limited palette: near-black obsidian, charcoal, muted ash violet, dull old gold, bone white, and one warm lamp-yellow light. Cool ash-violet darkness surrounds one warm pool of light. Painted flat backgrounds with no outlines and soft simplified edges away from the focal point; characters drawn with a thin dark charcoal line. Small warm lamp-coloured highlights in every eye. Quiet, still, atmospheric night. Restrained motion, slow camera. 9:16 vertical frame, main subject in the centre third. Vertical establishing view at night of Vesper Court, an old four-storey brick apartment block on a quiet residential street. Every window is dark except one on the third floor glowing warm lamp-yellow. One streetlamp on the left third throws a soft cone down the frame onto the empty pavement. Sky deep ash violet fading to near-black. Calm, lonely, beautiful and slightly wrong. Avoid: photorealism, 3D render, CGI, glossy plastic look, anime, chibi, bright or saturated colours, neon, multiple coloured light sources, heavy lens flare, blood, gore, wounds, corpses, monsters with faces, teeth or claws, text, letters, numbers, words, captions, subtitles, signage lettering, logos, brand names, watermarks, readable screens, extra fingers, distorted hands, people speaking or moving their lips, camera shake, fast cuts.
```
