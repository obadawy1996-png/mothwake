# MOTHWAKE Shorts Slate: 30 Concepts

**Status:** Creative Director proposal, 2026-11-19. Nothing here is approved. Owner review is required before any production or credit spend.
**Format:** 20–45 s vertical (9:16) Shorts, fully animated in Google Flow (Veo 3.1). Text, UI and captions are added in post. Kit: `ASSET_KIT.md`.
**Pillars:** 15 Micro Horror Stories · 9 Horror Rules & Survival · 6 Interactive Horror.

**How to read the numbers**
- **Priority** comes from `stories/SCORING_MODEL.md` (12 weighted criteria, 0–100). It is an **editorial estimate made before any performance data**. It ranks which Short to make next and does not predict views. `topic_demand` is linked to trend *categories* (research/WEEKLY_TREND_REPORT.md, measured 2026-11-19), not to the concept itself.
- **Credits** are **estimates** from the owner's brief: Lite 10 / Fast 20 per generation, and one retry per clip. The cap is 150 per Short. All 30 fit, in a range of 80–140. Not measured.
- **Similarity gate:** every file passed `scripts/story/storydb.py check`. The highest similarity to an existing story is 0.099 (SH020 vs MW010), with no WARN (≥0.30) or BLOCK (≥0.45). Within the slate, the highest pair is 0.230 (SH014 vs SH027), below the warn level. All 30 twist types are unique.
- **VO pace** is 0.9–1.8 words/s, deliberately sparse so that sound and image carry the dread. Every hook line is 10 words or fewer.

## Pilot picks (one per pillar)

| Pillar | Pick | Priority | Credits est. | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro Horror Story | **SH012 Rain Sounds** | 96.0 (slate #1) | 60 / 120 | The strongest hook-to-payoff ratio on the slate. Frame 1 (a sleep app with rain on the window) raises a question with no sound. The twist ("Duration: LIVE") recontextualises the very first second, so it is a true loop. It serves the sleep and waking gap (G4) and the tagline. Only one key moment (fast). The rest is simple actions in one room: REF-IVY, REF-E3-BEDROOM, REF-TENANT. |
| Horror Rules & Survival | **SH019 Theo's Nightlight Rules** | 91.9 (slate #2) | 60 / 120 | The purest expression of the brand metaphor: the light protects by holding the darkness's attention, like moths. Its child-written rules are our own structure, with no family "goodnight" device, so it is clearly distinct from the trending rules template. The payoff is a twist (a room full of figures facing the lamp), not a list. It shares SH012's bedroom and Tenant references, so set-up for pilot 2 is almost free. |
| Interactive Horror | **SH025 Two Nines** | 85.5 (top interactive) | 50 / 100 | A simple, fair-play "which door?" puzzle. The clue is visible in the first 5 s, and the answer is revealed in the same video, so there is no misleading deferral. It drives rewatches and timestamp comments. It is the cheapest of the three, and it adds the hallway (E1) to the kit for SH016 and SH029. SH028 (83.1) is the alternate. |
