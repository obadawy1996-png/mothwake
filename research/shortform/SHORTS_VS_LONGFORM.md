# MOTHWAKE: Short-form micro horror vs cinematic long-form

**Checked:** 2026-11-19. **Question:** Is the new 20–45 s micro-horror direction stronger than the original 8–14 min cinematic direction (e.g. EP001 *The Quiet Hour*, ~9:30–11:00)?
**Evidence:** `SHORTS_COMPETITORS.csv`, `GOOGLE_TRENDS.csv`, `YT_SEED_SHORTS.csv`, `SHORTS_FORMAT_ANALYSIS.md` (signals S1–S11), plus the earlier `../MARKET_OPPORTUNITY_REPORT.md`, `../PLATFORM_MONETIZATION.md`, `../../plans/PRODUCTION_COST_ANALYSIS.md` and `../../FINAL_PROJECT_HANDOVER.md`.
**Labels:** **measured** (public page today) · **official** (YouTube Help, from the earlier research) · **secondary** (press or vendor, via web-search snippets, not opened) · **estimate** (my arithmetic, assumptions stated).

## 2. Monetization arithmetic (estimates, not forecasts)

Assumptions are stated, and every input is cited above.

| Scenario | Inputs | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Shorts-only, hold the 10M/90-day gate** | 10 Shorts/month = 30 per 90 days. To reach 10M, each Short needs **≈333K views on average**. | Only **3 of 43** core channels in our sample have a recent median ≥333K (LIGHTS ARE OFF, UnwholesomePenguin, ParaHaunt). Revenue at a $0.03–$0.10 RPM on 10M views ≈ **$300–$1,000 per 90 days**. |
| **B. Shorts-only, reach the 20M/90-day entry bar** | 20M views in 90 days | ≈667K per Short at 30 Shorts. That is above every core channel's median except LIGHTS ARE OFF. |
| **C. Long-form, reach 8,000 watch hours / 365 days** | Assume a 10-min episode with a 4-min average view duration (assumption; retention unknown). | ≈120,000 long-form views in a year. That equals ~1.6% of one Lullorin-scale month (7.51M views). At the $2.30 median RPM, 1M long-form views ≈ $2,300, against ≈$30–$100 for 1M Shorts views. |
| **D. 1,000 subscribers** | 2.73 subs per 1K views (Shorts) vs 12.95 (long-form) | ≈366K Shorts views, or ≈77K long-form views, per 1,000 subs. |

**Most-viewed vs most-profitable.** Shorts produce the **most views**, fastest and cheapest per release. Long-form produces the **most revenue per view** (≈7–33× by the AIR 3–14% ratio), **more subscribers per view** (≈5×), the only realistic route to 2027 ad eligibility for a new channel, and search catalogue value. A Shorts-only MOTHWAKE could be highly viewed and still earn close to nothing from ads.

## 3. Recommendation

**Hybrid: Shorts-first for discovery and learning, with long-form kept as the revenue and brand engine. Do not run Shorts-only.**

1. **Run the 30-Short pilot as an experiment, not as the business model.** Its job is to find which hooks, topics and characters hold attention, cheaply. Use the ranked plan in `SHORTS_FORMAT_ANALYSIS.md` §7: sleep/waking and winter originals in Micro Horror, original-world protocol rules, and binary-dilemma serials with a comment vote.
2. **Keep long-form alive at a lower cadence** (about 1 episode every 2–4 weeks, 8–12 min). Each episode should **expand a Short that already performed**, for example a micro story whose twist people asked about, or a choice arc. That turns Short viewers into watch hours, which is what 2027 eligibility needs.
3. **Short-form is the stronger direction only under these conditions:** (a) the goal for the next 90 days is reach and learning, not revenue; (b) MOTHWAKE keeps each Short original and non-templated (the policy risk is real); (c) the series mechanics (rule numbers, two-part choices) are in place, so views turn into subscribers. If after 30 Shorts the subs-per-1K-views figure stays near the ~2.7 benchmark, and no Short beats about 3× the channel median, the long-form share should grow.
4. **Cinematic long-form is the stronger direction** if the goal is revenue within 12–18 months, a lasting world with fans and analysts, or licensing value. The measured long-form risers convert ≈5× better and earn ≈7–33× more per view (AIR ratio, secondary).
5. **Timing:** winter and Christmas original Shorts need to be live by about **2026-12-14**. Christmas horror interest on YouTube US peaked in the week of 2025-12-21 at ~13× its baseline.

**Decision marker for the owner:** the hybrid split (e.g. 30 Shorts plus 2 long-form episodes in the pilot window) is a recommendation, not a decision. **[APPROVAL NEEDED]**
