### Analysis
- **Hook:** a named character performing a dramatic act ("read out the best report card"), framed as last night's event. A report-card metaphor makes finance relatable.
- **Central question:** "Who really earned this record?" and the "fourteenth week" mystery (an accounting detail used as a cliffhanger).
- **Visual system:** flat vector chibi caricatures, one green-chalkboard palette, icon cards (calendar, clock), ticker strips, and **chapter title cards on black**. Interpretation: very template-friendly (cheap and fast), but long stretches of near-empty slate backgrounds (2:25, 5:20) suggest low visual density mid-video.
- **Personal relevance chapter:** "Your Wallet" turns corporate news into a viewer stake.
- **Ending:** an A/B decision card turns the audience into the board.
- **Packaging:** a two-sentence title with a reversal ("Best Quarter Ever. Then It Told Jensen Huang No.").
- **Risk:** it narrates real people and real companies with a contested storyline as fact. Not verified here. **Do not copy** this approach.
