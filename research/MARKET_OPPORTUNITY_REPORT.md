# MOTHWAKE: Market Opportunity Report (Phase 2)

**As of:** 2026-11-19 | **Scope:** English-language animated horror, psychological horror, mystery, urban legends, supernatural fiction, analog horror and short-form horror on YouTube.
**Data:** `COMPETITOR_DATABASE.csv` (63 channels), `TREND_DATABASE.csv` (42 rows), raw snapshots in `research/raw/`, sources in `RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

> **How to read the numbers.** Channel figures come from public YouTube pages on 2026-11-19. They are labelled **measured**, and YouTube itself rounds them (for example "461K"). Publish dates are YouTube's relative dates ("3w ago"). Press figures are labelled with their outlet. Anything I could not check is marked `unknown`. Third-party revenue estimates are **never** treated as income. No likes, comments, retention or CTR data were available (see Limits).

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The old wave of 2D "storytime" animation is fading. A new wave of high-concept, atmospheric originals is rising.** Legacy channels have big subscriber counts but small current reach. SNARLED (*Something Scary*) has 2.27M subscribers, yet its last 10 long-form videos have a median of only ~4.3K views. Llama Arts' last long-form upload was about a year ago. Meanwhile channels started in 2025–26 pull more views per video than they have subscribers: ParaHaunt (1.85×), The Glendale Archives (3.99×), Lullorin (2.38×) and Just a Rock in Space (2.17×). All are measured.
2. **What the risers share:** one clear premise per video, a strong title "rule" or hook, low gore, and one consistent world or format. They do **not** use the generic "N TRUE horror stories ANIMATED" compilation. New entrants in that template sit at 0.01–0.17× views/subs.
3. **Search for English psychological 2D animation is thin at the top.** Generic "animated horror" searches are led by Hindi and Bengali channels, and English story-led psychological animation rarely appears in the top 20. That is the lane MOTHWAKE fits.
4. **Internet horror is crossing into cinema in 2026.** *Iron Lung* opened 2026-01-30 ($18.19M domestic opening, as reported). A24's *Backrooms* opened 2026-05-29 and set an A24 record. *The Mandela Catalogue* film is dated 2028-06-09. The genre's cultural weight is rising, and so is the value of owning **original** worlds.
5. **The main risks are platform policy and saturation, not demand.** These are YouTube's "inauthentic content" rules, which target mass-produced or templated videos, an early-2026 crackdown on AI slop (as reported), and reported tougher Partner Program thresholds for new applicants from 2027-02-01. A long tail of near-empty "rules horror" channels shows how fast a format gets flooded.

### 2.2 Market structure observations (measured, YouTube search, 2026-11-19)
- **Non-English dominance in generic searches.** Top results for "animated horror story" are Scary Pumpkin (Hindi: 6.48M, 4.99M, 2.48M views this month) and Horror Tales (Hindi: 3.17M). Three of the top four results for "psychological horror animation" are Bengali channels.
- **Game and streamer noise.** "liminal", "mimic", "backrooms" and "rules" searches are crowded with gameplay (The Classrooms, Roblox) and big general creators. Story-led English fiction is a minority there.
- **The format is flooded at the bottom.** In a channel search for "rules horror story", 11 of the 20 channels returned showed 133 subscribers or fewer (many 1–3) and 3 more showed no subscriber count (raw/yt_channel_search_2026-11-19_b.json). The same pattern appears for "2d horror animation" and "dark fairy tale animated".
- **Seasonal timing.** In the "this month" filter on 2026-11-19, Christmas and winter-folklore horror has very little supply: "christmas horror story" median 8.7K, "analog horror christmas" median 201, "winter folklore horror" median 263. The exception is Mr. Nightmare's winter-adjacent uploads: "Cold Night" 724K and "Home Alone" 794K, both within the last 8 days.

## 3. What works and why

Measured signals are labelled; the "why" is **qualitative inference** from titles, formats and outcomes. It is not retention data.

| Driver | Evidence | Implication for MOTHWAKE |
|---|---|---|
| **Watch (click)**: a single uncanny premise, stated plainly | Lullorin "If Your Mom Ever Says Goodnight to You Twice... LOCK YOUR DOOR" 3.3M in ~3 weeks. Glendale "The world is empty. Everything is mine." 1.1M. MrBallen's plain-sentence hooks (median ~1.2M). | One idea per title, written in ordinary words, with no list numbers. |
| **Continue (retention)**: atmosphere and a slow reveal, not gore | Low-gore risers dominate (Glendale, Lullorin, Thriller Teller). Analysis channels praise "loneliness" (Crowmudgeon "The Loneliest World in Analog Horror" 2.2M). | MOTHWAKE's dark, psychological, low-gore brief is an asset. Use the length to build dread. |
| **Rewatch**: hidden clues and lore | The analog-horror community theorises (Roar News 2024). Explainer channels such as Nexpo, Crowmudgeon and soda girl make millions of views from other creators' lore. | Plant fair-play clues and recurring symbols (the moth, the light), so episodes reward a second look and analysts have something to cover. |
| **Share**: a relatable everyday scenario made wrong | Lullorin's home-at-night premises (bed already warm, night light, voicemail). Ultis "Watching TikTok's Most Horrifying Videos" 1.6M. | Base fear on familiar objects and routines: lamps, windows, alarms, sleep. |
| **Subscribe**: a series identity or world | GREYLOCK 121K subscribers from 12 tapes. White Stag 38.2K from 5 videos. Glendale's serial logs. Mandela/Backrooms universes. | A recurring world ("the Wake") with standalone episodes and a slow seasonal arc. |
| **Trust**: human-made positioning | Just a Rock in Space: "100% HUMAN MADE ANIMATIONS" and "[NO AI]" in titles, 368K subs from 16 videos. Mort's About page: "No automated content generation". | Disclose production honestly and say plainly that the stories are original. |

### Formats and durations (measured medians, last 10 long-form)
- Risers cluster at **~8–14 minutes**: Lullorin 12:00, Just a Rock in Space 11:02, Thriller Teller 10:06, Merrily 10:14, Animated Horror Flicks 8:24, Surkee 9:03, GREYLOCK 8:57.
- Exceptions: Glendale runs **27:20** (serial logs) and ParaHaunt **4:02** (creature listicles, heavy on Shorts).
- Narration channels run long (Lighthouse Horror 48:19, Insomnia Stories 1:22:02), and so do compilations (Dr. NoSleep 1:00:11, Urban Legends Animated 1:56:27).
- **Shorts**: Shorts-tab medians are high for creature and atmosphere channels (ParaHaunt ~689K, Stretch ~333K, Liminal Static ~253K). SCP Explained Shorts run ~4× its long-form median. Shorts monetization thresholds are reported to be tightening (see §6), so treat Shorts mainly as discovery.

## 5. Where MOTHWAKE fits

**Positioning:** *English-language, human-directed, original 2D animated psychological horror and mystery. Quiet dread, little gore, and a recurring world where things are drawn to an unknown light.*

- **Lane:** original standalone 8–14 min episodes. Measured evidence favours this length for story-led risers. Each episode has one plain-language premise and a recurring "Wake" mythology that rewards rewatching.
- **Differentiators:** (1) original IP. No Reddit adaptations, no SCP or Backrooms lore, no third-party creatures. (2) The moth/light motif is almost unclaimed: "moth horror" had a top-20 median of ~1.9K views this month. (3) Honest human-made positioning. (4) A low-gore psychological tone that fits the space left by fading compilation channels.
- **Tone match by trend:** isolation/emptiness (fit 5), winter and holiday originals (5), sleep and waking horror (5), psychological shorts (5), original mysteries with clues (4), liminal settings (4), analog devices used inside 2D animation (4).
- **Avoid:** "N TRUE horror stories ANIMATED" compilations, skinwalker/wendigo (saturated and culturally sensitive), Japanese-legend retellings (crowded), Backrooms/SCP IP, copying the "If your mom..." title template word for word, and any "moths at the porch light, don't open the door" premise (an existing video uses one).

## 6. Top risks

| Risk | Evidence | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inauthentic (mass-produced or repetitive) content and loss of YPP eligibility** | YouTube renamed "repetitious" to "inauthentic content" on 2025-07-15. Reused-content policy unchanged. Enforcement looks at titles, thumbnails and descriptions as well as the videos (Search Engine Journal, PPC Land, Gulf News). | High | Every episode gets a unique script, art, edit and thumbnail. No near-duplicate templates or bulk uploads. |
| **Reused content** | Reused-content policy unchanged (press, 2025-07). Shorts cut from your *own* episodes are fine, but clips of others are not. | Medium | Use only original assets, licensed music and SFX, and keep a rights log per episode. |
| **AI-slop association** | Early-2026 removal of AI-slop channels reported by Tubefilter (2026-01-29). Creators now advertise "[NO AI]". | High (reputational) | Disclose any AI assistance honestly. The human-directed story and art direction must be visible. |
| **YPP threshold rise for new applicants (2027-02-01)** | Reported: 1,000 subs plus 8,000 qualified watch hours (365 days) or 20M Shorts views (90 days). Announced 2026-08-10 (PPC Land, Social Media Today, Hypebot). **Not checked on YouTube Help (blocked).** | High for timing | Plan for long-form watch time. Verify the official terms before Phase 8. |
| **Format saturation** | Long tail of near-empty "rules" channels; compilation template ratios 0.01–0.17. | Medium | Differentiate on world, tone and craft rather than format alone. |
| **IP and cultural risk** | Risers using third-party IP (Kris Archives, ParaHaunt). Skinwalker and wendigo draw on Indigenous belief. | Medium | Original creatures only. Run a cultural sensitivity check before using any real folklore. |
| **Demand estimates are thin** | No Google Trends, Reddit, retention or CTR data in this phase. | Medium | Re-run with API access (see RESEARCH_LOG). Treat early episodes as experiments. |

## 7. Limits of this research
- There are no likes, comments, retention, CTR or traffic-source data. YouTube watch pages redirect to a blocked host, and there is no YouTube Data API key.
- Thumbnails could not be viewed (i.ytimg.com blocked), so thumbnail patterns are inferred from titles only.
- YouTube search results are not geo-controlled, and each was captured once. "Direction" is mostly qualitative.
- Google Trends, Reddit, Social Blade, YouTube Help, Google's blog and NexLev were blocked. The details are in RESEARCH_LOG.md.
