## Analysis (against the brief's 8 focus points)
1. **Recurring characters:** simple round-head cartoon people. Real people are signalled by a few traits (glasses, hairstyle, sunglasses) plus name tags and captions, not detailed likeness. A recurring "analyst at a night desk" anchors the investigations (seen in both the 11:40 and 19:30 frames).
2. **Real businesspeople:** shown as simplified caricatures and named in captions. Legal and ethical risk is high when the storyline is unverified (see the conflict above).
3. **Illustration style:** flat vector "paper" look, dark night palettes, desk-lamp pools of light, hand-lettered labels, paper-collage headline boards, red-string evidence boards. **It's closest to our MOTHWAKE mood** (night, one warm light).
4. **Simplifying complexity:** physical metaphors (org chart as a building, the reporting wheel "57", counters on a calendar, boxes on a conveyor, a man walking a stock chart).
5. **Packaging:** "How a [villain] Got [inside/$X] [famous company]" / "Why [company] Would Fail Without [person]": a named villain or a named hero + a big number.
6. **Pacing and structure:** a cold open on the most dramatic moment (the spy dinner) → "to see why that mattered, you need to know who…" → chaptered long-form (14–28 min), ending on an open question ("Who should have warned him?").
7. **Recognition:** the consistent cartoon style, hand-lettered cards, paper-headline boards and the night-desk analyst.
8. **Can we recreate it efficiently?** The *style* is simple vector (cheaper than Flow video). But copying it would be imitation, and **its speed comes from a fully automated AI pipeline**, which is exactly what YouTube's "inauthentic / mass-produced" policy targets and what the MOTHWAKE brief rules out ("We are not building a factory").
