# Competitor channel audit: tavieenfilm and Money Decoded

**Inspected:** 2026-11-20 in the built-in browser (YouTube, Arabic UI). **Method:** channel pages, video metadata (title, length, public view count, publish date, description, chapters), and **sampled still frames** taken by seeking the player to set timestamps. Burned-in captions in those frames give partial narration.
**Not done:** watching full videos with sound, or reading full transcripts (captions are not offered as a track; see `research_limitations.md`). Retention, CTR and demographics are private: **unavailable**.

## Reading the numbers
- Only public counts were used, and they're a single snapshot: they say nothing about retention or long-term success.
- tavieenfilm: one breakout (3.1M in about 10 days), one strong video (934K), and one recent upload (77K at 3 days). That's a strong but very young track record; don't treat it as a stable baseline.
- Money Decoded is too small, with too few public videos, to draw performance conclusions from. Use it for **craft** lessons only.
