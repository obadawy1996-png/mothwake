## Autocomplete (US, YouTube) signals worth using
- "rain sounds horror **story**", "rain sounds **scary stories**" (exists, sleep audience)
- "babysitter horror story **animated / rules**", "neighbor horror story **animated**", "haunted apartment **cheap rent**"
- "2 sentence horror story **animated**", "horror rules **to follow / to survive / stories**"
- "which door would you choose **scary / horror / backrooms**", "pick a door horror"
- "something **is knocking at my window**", "creepy text messages **stories**", "scary facts **about dreams**"
- "christmas horror story **animated**", "winter horror **stories**"
- Lots of autocomplete includes "**true**" ("animated horror stories true", "scary story animated true"). Avoid it: MOTHWAKE is fiction (no misleading claims).

## Meta Ad Library (US, active video ads)
- "scary stories": **~1,900 results**, dominated by **AI faceless-video tools** (FacelessReels, Autoclips, several individual advertisers): "type your niche… connect TikTok, Instagram, YouTube"; "AI created it from one typed idea and posted it while I slept".
- "horror story animation": **Norveko** (an AI app) advertises "an interactive horror story… Comment A or B. The most voted choice becomes Part 2", with a night-shift rules premise.
- "horror animation": **lullabitestales**, "Sixty seconds… Would you put her down?" (dilemma CTA); Blender Lovecraftian art (guoshun_vfx).
- **Read:** the horror Shorts space on Meta is being flooded by automated tools, and the "rules + A/B vote" format is now an app template. Generic = invisible; **recognisable craft, a recurring world and characters are the moat.**

## Cover/thumbnail patterns on the top Shorts (observed on the shelf)
One central character or creature, close; a 2–4 word punch line burned on the cover ("IT CAN", "HE WASN'T A CLOWN", "SKINWALKER AT DINNER"); high contrast with one hot accent (red/white); faces with clear expressions; stylised 2D/3D craft, not photoreal.
