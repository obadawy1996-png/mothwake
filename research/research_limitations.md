# Research limitations (competitor study, 2026-11-20)

1. **No full viewing with sound.** Videos were inspected by seeking the player to set timestamps and capturing still frames (about 7 per video). Narration was read only from the **burned-in captions** visible in those frames. Music, voice quality and shot-by-shot pacing were **not** observed.
2. **No transcripts.** YouTube's player reported "captions unavailable", and an attempt to read the transcript through YouTube's internal API was blocked by the safety classifier as unauthorised access. It was not retried. *To unlock:* the owner can paste transcripts (YouTube "Show transcript" on desktop) or provide authorised copies of the videos.
3. **Two of three tavieenfilm videos** were analysed from title, description and chapters only (no frames).
4. **Money Decoded:** only 1 of the "6" videos is publicly listed now. The titles from the owner's screenshot couldn't be inspected.
5. **Private analytics** (retention, CTR, traffic sources, demographics) are unavailable for both channels. The public view counts are a single snapshot.
6. **Unverified facts:** the Money Decoded storyline and the tavieenfilm study citations were not fact-checked.
7. **Language:** the YouTube UI was in Arabic; counts were converted from Arabic notation (e.g. "3 مليون" = 3,098,436 exact from the page data).
