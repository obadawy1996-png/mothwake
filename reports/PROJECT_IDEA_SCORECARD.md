# MOTHWAKE — Project Idea Scorecard

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Overall: 66 / 100 — promising, but it depends on execution.**

This is a judgement drawn from the Phase 2 research (`research/`). It is not a forecast of views or income.

| Dimension | Weight | Score /10 | Why (evidence) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market opportunity | 20% | **8** | New-wave 2D horror channels started in 2025–26 get more views than subscribers. Examples: Glendale Archives (views-to-subs ratio 3.99), ParaHaunt (1.85), Lullorin (3.36M views in about 3 weeks). Meanwhile the older storytime channels are fading, and English psychological 2D animation is thin at the top of search. |
| Brand distinctiveness | 15% | **8** | The moth and light identity is nearly unclaimed ("moth horror" top-20 median about 1.9K views). There is a full brand kit, and the logo has a strong double read. |
| Format fit vs. what's working | 15% | **6** | The winners use one clear premise, a plain-sentence title, run **8–14 min**, and build a consistent world. The plan's 5–10 min long-form target is short of that. The generic "N true stories animated" format is saturated. |
| Monetization path | 15% | **5** | From 2027-02-01, new channels need 1,000 subscribers plus **8,000 watch hours** or **20M Shorts views in 90 days**. Shorts earn only in months with 10M Shorts views over the previous 90 days. A plan that leans on Shorts reaches ad revenue more slowly. The fan-funding tier (500 subscribers) is the realistic first milestone. |
| Production feasibility and cost | 15% | **6** | The FFmpeg, Python and brand pipeline works locally at no cost. But publishable output needs **paid** plans, because the ElevenLabs, OpusClip and Magnific free tiers are non-commercial and OpusClip's free tier adds a watermark. Google Flow has no API and allows only 3 reference images per clip. Resolve Free can't be scripted. |
| Policy and platform risk (higher = safer) | 10% | **5** | YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule applies to the whole channel, and AI-slop channels were removed in early 2026. Audiences are rewarding "[NO AI] / human-made" positioning. Thirty AI-assisted Shorts a month could look mass-produced. |
| Defensibility and originality | 10% | **7** | An original story engine with a similarity gate, a recurring motif and world, and a scoring model. The defensibility is the house style, which competitors can't easily copy. |

Weighted total: 0.2×8 + 0.15×8 + 0.15×6 + 0.15×5 + 0.15×6 + 0.1×5 + 0.1×7 = **6.55, rounded to 66/100.**

## What would raise the score (actions already being applied)

1. **Make long-form the core:** 8–14 min complete stories for the watch-hour route. Shorts act as discovery trailers for them, not the revenue engine.
2. **Fewer, better Shorts:** about 12–15 in the pilot instead of 30, each with a distinct story. That protects against the inauthentic-content rule.
3. **Show the human craft:** hand-directed animation, original voice direction, visible process content. Use AI as a tool, not the product.
4. **Seasonal window:** one original winter story live before 1 December, when supply is very low.
5. **A recurring world** (the Moth, the town of Wrenfield) to turn viewers into returning viewers.

## What would lower it

- Publishing templated or near-identical videos.
- Chasing Backrooms or rules trends with copies instead of originals.
- Relying on Shorts revenue in 2027.
- Spending on paid plans before a pilot proves people keep watching.

## Concept collisions found by research (actioned)

- `MW001 The Porch Light Rule` overlaps an existing 2026 video built on "moths at a porch light + don't open the door". It is **demoted and needs a premise rework** before scripting.

## Path to 100/100

A perfect score can't be reached on paper. Policy and monetization rules are outside MOTHWAKE's control, and the final ~15 points can only be earned with **proof**: real retention, returning viewers and subscribers from the pilot. The table shows what moves each dimension to 9–10.

| Dimension | Now | Target | What gets it there | Earned by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Market opportunity | 8 | 10 | Own a lane nobody holds: **English psychological 2D horror with a recurring world**. Launch a winter original before 1 Dec, while supply is very low. Re-scan weekly and move fast on gaps. | Plan + speed |
| Brand distinctiveness | 8 | 10 | One **recurring world** (the town of Wrenfield, the Moth as a hidden easter egg in every story) and a signature visual device (the Lamp-eye highlight). Viewers should recognise a MOTHWAKE frame with the logo hidden. | Execution |
| Format fit | 6 | 9–10 | Long-form at **8–14 min** with one clear premise and a plain-sentence title. Shorts act as 35–60 s trailers or self-contained stories that point to the long-form. Drop compilations. | Plan change (proposed below) |
| Monetization path | 5 | 8–9 | Make **watch hours** the main route: 8,000 hours in 365 days, for example 12 long-form stories × about 670 hours each. Reach the 500-subscriber fan-funding tier first (memberships, Super Thanks). Build owned revenue later (digital story collections, licensing the world). | Pilot data |
| Production feasibility | 6 | 9 | Build a **reusable asset library** (backgrounds, poses, characters) so each new episode costs mainly writing, voice and direction. Keep the stack lean: ElevenLabs Starter or Creator plus Flow AI Pro. Use Resolve Free by hand plus the in-house FFmpeg pipeline. Measure cost per minute. | Phases 5–7 + pilot |
| Policy risk | 5 | 9 | **A human-made signature:** original scripts, directed voice, hand-composed layouts, and behind-the-scenes process clips. Disclose AI where required. Never templated batches; every video has a distinct story (the similarity gate enforces this). Publish at a steady, sustainable rate. | Discipline |
| Defensibility | 7 | 10 | An owned universe (characters, places, lore), a story bible, and recurring characters audiences ask for. A world can be licensed; a trend format can't. | Time + audience |

**Ceiling today:** about 85–90/100 once the plan changes are adopted. The rest comes from the 30-day pilot: one long-form story beating its cohort median, Shorts with a "viewed vs swiped" rate above the channel's own baseline, and a measurable returning-viewer rate.

**One tool reality check:** OpusClip's AI Producer is built for talking-head footage under about 5 minutes. For MOTHWAKE its value is limited to cutting Shorts from long-form, captions and reframing. It should not be the core editor.
