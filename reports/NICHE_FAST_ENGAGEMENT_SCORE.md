# Is 2D micro horror Shorts a good niche for FAST engagement?

**Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Data:** `research/shortform/SHORTS_COMPETITORS.csv` (47 channels, measured 2026-11-19, RSS exact counts), `raw/title_feature_lift_2026-11-19.json` (476 Shorts), `SHORTS_FORMAT_ANALYSIS.md`. Computed in this session from those files. No new data was collected.
**Labels:** every number is **measured** (source above) unless marked *judgement*.

## Verdict: 74/100 for fast engagement (judgement). It's good, but only for a specific style.
This niche can produce early engagement in days, not months. The results are **split, though**. New channels with an original format and a real hook break out; channels copying templates stay near zero. Getting fast engagement depends on doing it the right way, not just on being in the niche.

## Limits
- There's no Meta, Instagram or TikTok data. Those sites were blocked during the research, so this score is **YouTube Shorts only**. Real Meta numbers come from your own account insights after posting.
- Pillar medians for the small groups (n=1–2) are weak evidence.
- This tells us where to bet. **It is not a forecast** of MOTHWAKE's views.
